This is a fictional sample posting written for the Groundwork demo. Quillmoss Analytics does not exist.

About us:
Quillmoss Analytics helps mid-sized retailers understand what sells, where, and why. Our team of twelve analysts and engineers turns point-of-sale data into weekly reports that store managers actually read.

What you'll do:
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for sell-through, weeks of supply and stock variance
- Write SQL queries against our PostgreSQL warehouse to answer questions from client teams
- Clean and reshape raw sales extracts with Python and pandas before they reach the warehouse
- Check every weekly report against last week's numbers and flag anything that moved more than expected
- Present one finding to the client team at the end of the term

What you bring:
- Enrolled in a computer science, statistics, business analytics or related program
- Comfortable writing SQL joins and aggregations
- Some experience with Python, including pandas
- Clear written English; you'll write short notes that explain each dashboard change

Nice to have:
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Familiarity with retail terms such as SKU, sell-through and replenishment

Work arrangement:
This is a hybrid role. You'll work from our Toronto office on Tuesdays and Thursdays and from home the rest of the week. We provide a laptop.

Term and pay:
The co-op runs from January 4, 2027 to April 30, 2027, full time at 37.5 hours per week. The pay range is $24 to $27 per hour.

How to apply:
Apply through your school's co-op portal with a resume and a short note about a dataset you enjoyed working with. We'll invite shortlisted students to a 30-minute video interview.
