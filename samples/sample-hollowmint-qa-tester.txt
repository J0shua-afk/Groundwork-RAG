Hollowmint Games is a small studio making puzzle games for phones. Our next game, a tile-sliding puzzler with 300 hand-built levels, launches on iOS and Android this spring, and we need help finding bugs before players do.

As our QA Tester Co-op you will play builds of the game every day on a range of iOS and Android devices, write clear bug reports in Jira with steps to reproduce, screenshots and device details, re-test fixes before they go out, and check every level on our list before each release. Later in the term you can help write automated tests in C# with the Unity Test Framework if you're interested.

You should be enrolled in a co-op program in computer science, game development or a related field, enjoy puzzle games, and be patient enough to play the same level fifty times to find out exactly when a bug happens. Some experience with Unity or C# is an asset but not required.

The term is Winter 2027, January to April. Exact start and end dates will be confirmed with your school. Pay is $19.50 per hour, full time.

Apply through your co-op portal with a resume and a short list of three mobile games you've played recently and one thing you'd change about each.
